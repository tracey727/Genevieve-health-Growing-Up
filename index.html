How does this feel?

YOU ARE NOT IN TROUBLE

Stop and get support.

Move away from anything unsafe. Put razors, medicines or other objects down. Go to a trusted adult or safe public place.

Immediate danger or serious injury

Contact emergency services now or ask an adult nearby to do it.

A body or health concern

Tell a parent, carer, GP, pharmacist, school nurse or another qualified health professional.

Someone crossed a boundary

Tell a trusted adult. Do not keep secrets about unsafe touching, threats or private images. Keep telling until someone helps.

You are frightened of your thoughts

Stay with a safe person and seek urgent professional or crisis support.
